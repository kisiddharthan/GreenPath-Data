Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

ImmiGreen helps users track employment-based U.S. Visa Bulletin updates. Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

ImmiGreen stores your immigration profile locally on your device. We do not require account registration.

Information you enter may include:

  • Country of Chargeability
  • Employment-based category
  • Priority Date

Data Collection

We do not collect personal information.

We do not sell data.

We do not share data with advertisers.

Internet Access

The app downloads publicly available Visa Bulletin information from the U.S. Department of State.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, ImmiGreen may notify you when new Visa Bulletin information becomes available.

Your Data

All saved immigration information remains on your device unless you delete it.
